# Eye-blinking in wolves and dogs

Raw behavioural data supporting the manuscript **“Human interaction and social bond modulate eye-blink rate and timing in wolves and dogs.”**

The study examines blink rates and the timing of animal blinks relative to human blinks in pet dogs, pack-living dogs and wolves during greetings with bonded and familiar humans, alongside a non-social control condition.

Data were obtained through new coding of videos originally collected for [Capitain et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10071-025-01978-7). 
See the manuscript for coding definitions and analytical methods.
